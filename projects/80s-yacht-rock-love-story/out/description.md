"The Long Way Home" is a yacht rock love story set on the coast in the late 1980s, built as a love letter to the soft rock and yacht rock sound of the era.

The video follows one couple across a single golden evening. It opens at a sunlit marina, where two strangers trade a first glance across a waterside bar. From there, the story moves down the coast: a convertible cruise along the cliffs as the sun drops toward the water, a candlelit dinner on a pier restaurant strung with warm lights, a slow dance barefoot in the sand, and a closing embrace under the stars with sailboats resting on the dark water below.

There is no dialogue and no narration - the story is told entirely through the visuals and the music, the way a yacht rock album cover or a soft-focus 1980s music video would tell it. Every frame was built to feel like it was actually shot in 1987: period-correct convertibles, pastel Art-Deco resort architecture, and warm 35mm film grain rather than a modern, digital look.

This is the first video in a new direction for the channel: nostalgic music videos built around a single song and a single story, rather than narrated documentary content. If you enjoy the warm, unhurried, sun-drenched mood of soft jazz yacht rock and the visual style of 1980s coastal romance, this one is for you.
